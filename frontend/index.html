Crafted for Indian Realities

Three Core Pillars of Vaani

Designed specifically for kirana owners, small businessmen, and families who run their lives on trust, memory, and spoken words.

Bol-Khata

Digital Business Ledger

No more lost paper notebooks or mental stress. Just speak naturally to record udhaar (credit) and jama (payments) in seconds.

  • Fuzzy Customer Matching: Maps "रमेश", "Ramesh", "Rameshh", "Ramesh ji" accurately.
  • Paise Precision: Integer math prevents float drift. Never makes accounting rounding errors.
  • 8-Second Voice Undo: Say "Galat ho gaya, hata do" or tap Undo to safely revert.

Parchi

Medicine Reminders

Keep family elder care effortless. Speak the doctor's prescription, and Vaani schedules spoken alerts for every dose.

  • Verbatim Safety: Medicine names and doses stored strictly as spoken without alteration.
  • Smart Meal Slots: Automatically maps subah, shaam, dopahar, and khaane ke baad.
  • Strict Medical Refusal: Declines medical diagnosis or dosage changes by design.

Sensex Awaaz

Market Briefing

Stay on top of your mutual funds and portfolio with spoken, jargon-free market overviews in your mother tongue.

  • Plain Language: "Sensex aaj 342 point upar band hua" instead of complex financial jargon.
  • Zero Investment Advice: Declines stock picks or buy/sell predictions immediately.
  • Honest Freshness: Clear timestamped badges ("Live" vs "Sample Data").
Sovereign Indian AI Stack

How Vaani Executes Every Turn

Python validates, calculates, and writes the database. The language models only understand.

STAGE 01

Audio Capture

Web Audio / MediaRecorder

Shopkeeper voice captured in browser, streamed or converted to 16 kHz mono WAV.

STAGE 02

Speech to Text

Gnani Prisma v3.0

Automatic language detection handles code-mixed speech, Hindi, Marathi, and Hinglish flawlessly.

STAGE 03

Intent Reasoning

Gnani Evon v3.3 (30B)

Outputs strictly validated JSON action payloads. Never touches database directly.

STAGE 04

Guardrails & DB

Python 3.10 + SQLite WAL

Validates limits, runs integer-paise math, writes local SQLite ledger with full rollback support.

STAGE 05

Spoken Reply

Gnani Timbre v2.5

Synthesizes warm, respectful audio replies with confirmed numbers in Hindi, Marathi, or English.
